FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to what most people ask before we work together.

What does a Kajabi audit cover?

Your site, funnel, checkout, offers, automations and integrations. You get a clear list of what's missing, what's broken and where sales are leaking.

Can I migrate from WordPress or ClickFunnels without losing SEO?

Yes. Content, page structure and redirects are planned before the move so your rankings and traffic carry over.

How long does a build take?

It depends on scope. A migration can be live in under a week; a full rebuild with funnel, course and automations takes longer. I confirm a timeline after the audit.

What do I need to start?

An active Kajabi account (or a plan to open one), your branding, and your offer details. I'll guide you through the rest.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes: updates, fixes, tweaks and a check on your automations after launch.

Which integrations do you support?

Zapier, Stripe and PayPal, plus other third-party tools, connected and tested properly.
